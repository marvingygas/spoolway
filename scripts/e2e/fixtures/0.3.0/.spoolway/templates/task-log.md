# Task log

What a lane writes into its task file. One `##` section per place spoolway
appends. The three headings below are spoolway's own — it appends under
those names and no others — and the prose under each is yours. Leave a
section out and spoolway writes nothing under that heading at all. Delete
this whole file and every section falls back to spoolway's own wording.

Spoolway supplies the timestamp, the arrow and the step credit; everything
below is what the lane itself has to say.

## Status Log

One line per transition, in behaviour rather than in file names. Somebody
scanning the board reads this and nothing else, so write it for them.

## Handoff

Anything the next step needs and the diff does not show: what you tried and
abandoned, a call site you found, a constraint that made the obvious
approach wrong. One entry per thing. If your step judges work, its findings
go here too — a file, a line, and what would make it right.

## Blocker

What is in the way, what you tried, and what somebody would have to do to
get past it. Not a theory of the cause.
